# AWS Support Plans - Architectural Guidance

**Date**: 2026-05-11

## So sánh Architectural Guidance

| Plan | Architectural Guidance | Support Access |
|------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Basic** | ❌ Không có | Customer service only |
| **Developer** | ❌ General only (không specific) | Email (business hours) |
| **Business** | ✅ **Contextual to use-cases** | 24x7 phone/email/chat |
| **Enterprise** | ✅ Contextual to use-cases + TAM | 24x7 + TAM |

## Business Support Plan

**Minimum plan** có **contextual architectural guidance**

**Features**:
- ✅ Architectural guidance contextual to specific use-cases
- ✅ 24x7 phone, email, chat support
- ✅ Full access to AWS Trusted Advisor checks
- ✅ Infrastructure Event Management (additional fee)
- ✅ Recommended for **production workloads**

## Developer Support Plan

- ❌ Chỉ có **general architectural guidance** (không contextual)
- Email support (business hours only)
- 7 core Trusted Advisor checks
- Không có Infrastructure Event Management

## Enterprise Support Plan

- ✅ Contextual architectural guidance (như Business)
- ✅ **Technical Account Manager (TAM)**
- ✅ Infrastructure Event Management (included)
- ✅ Proactive/preventative programs
- Đắt hơn Business nhiều

## Lưu ý khi làm bài thi

Câu hỏi: **"Which plan provides X?"** (singular)
→ Chọn **minimum/cheapest plan** có feature đó

- Business = **Minimum plan** có contextual guidance ✅
- Enterprise cũng có, nhưng không phải minimum requirement
